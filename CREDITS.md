# Sound credits

Mic Party is a just-for-fun project for friends. It is not sold and has no ads.

- **Meme clips** come from https://www.myinstants.com. They are short excerpts of internet memes and belong to their original owners. If you own one and want it removed, open an issue and it will be taken out.
- **Google Sound Library** - https://developers.google.com/assistant/tools/sound-library (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **BigSoundBank** (Joseph Sardin) - https://bigsoundbank.com (CC0)

All clips were trimmed and loudness-normalized by `tools/prepare_sounds.py`.
